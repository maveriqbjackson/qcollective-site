What we are

Nonpartisan and citizen-run. No party, no candidate, no endorsements.

02

What we do

We track what lawmakers actually do and score every one of them the same way, with the Q Score. We show our work in a public audit log. We started with all 100 Colorado lawmakers.

03

Every state, plus Washington, D.C. and the U.S. territories: Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa and the Northern Mariana Islands.
