The Citizen’s Oath

I put citizens first.

I put my country and my neighbors before any party.

I will never bow to extremism — from the right, the left, or anywhere it hides.

I reject political violence, intimidation, and the lie that my neighbor is my enemy.

I will treat those I disagree with as fellow citizens — with respect they may not return.

I will stay informed, show up, and hold every official to the same standard, no matter their party.

I will speak softly and carry a big stick: reason over rage, truth over noise.

Where we started does not matter. Where we finish — together — does.
