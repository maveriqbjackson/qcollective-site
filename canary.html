What a canary is

Coal miners used to bring a canary into the mine. If the air turned dangerous, the canary went quiet first, and everyone knew to get out. Nobody had to say a word.

Ours works the same way. A government agency can send an organization a secret order, like a subpoena for its records or a demand for its supporters' information, and attach a gag order that makes it illegal to tell anyone. Nobody can stop that from happening. But nobody can force us to keep saying something that isn't true, either.

So we promise in public, every 7 days, that it hasn't happened. If that promise stops being renewed, you'll know something changed, even if we're not allowed to tell you what.

How to read it

Green: signed and currentRenewed within the last 7 days. Nothing has happened that we're forbidden from telling you about.
Yellow: due for renewalIt's been more than 7 days. Usually that just means a busy week. Check back in a day or two.
Red: overdueMore than 10 days with no renewal. Treat that as the message.

Your own browser works out the color from the date of the last signing. We don't run that check on our servers, so there's no switch anyone could quietly turn off. If nobody signs, the page goes red on your phone, not ours.

Who signs it

A small group of people we trust, each with their own sign-in. Every signing is dated, shown below, and logged in our public audit log. A signer can only renew the exact statement above. Nobody can change its wording from the signing page.

Every signer has one instruction: if any part of the statement might no longer be true, or if they've been told not to say so, don't sign. Let it lapse.

Why some signings say “A signer.” Our founder, maveriQ B Jackson, signs under his own name. He puts his name and his credibility behind every word of this statement. Everyone else who signs shows as “A signer.” They’re private people who volunteered to help keep this honest, and naming them would make them a target for pressure. Protecting them is part of what keeps the canary trustworthy.
