The 2026 results: Colorado and the nation

Who won, what's still being counted, and what changes when the new lawmakers take office in January.

Latest counts we could confirm, from Wednesday evening to Thursday morning, November 5. Several races are still being counted and nothing is official until each state certifies. We'll update this page as races are called, and every number links back to where we got it.

On Tuesday, Democrats won control of the U.S. House and the U.S. Senate, picked up governorships across the country, swept Colorado's statewide offices, and won a two-thirds supermajority in Colorado's state House. Here's the full picture, with the numbers and where each one comes from.

The national tally

U.S. House
240 D · 147 R

48 seats still counting. 218 is a majority, so Democrats have already won the House.

U.S. Senate
54 D · 44 R

Counts the two independents who vote with Democrats. Alaska and Mississippi are still counting. Either way, Democrats hold the majority.

Governors
6 flipped

Democrats flipped Alaska, Georgia, Iowa, Nevada, Ohio and Texas. Republicans flipped none. Kansas is still counting.

What this means for our work

A lot of new names. Colorado gets a new governor, attorney general, secretary of state and treasurer, plus new members of Congress. The legislature is back on January 13, 2027. Every new lawmaker gets a Q Score once they start voting, scored on the same seven pillars as everyone else, whichever party they're in.

The bills don't change because the party math did. CESA and FWMRA still need sponsors, and we'll ask newcomers and veterans on both sides. We'll also read every measure that passes against our bills and publish anything that changes.

Going national. The new Congress is sworn in on January 3, 2027. We plan to score every member of Congress and every governor we can get solid public data on, starting with Colorado's delegation. Here's how we're doing it.

We don't endorse candidates or parties. We publish the record, on behalf of the people of the United States. We have no client and no money riding on the outcome.
