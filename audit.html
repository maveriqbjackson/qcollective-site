Every change to every score, on the record. A score is never a black box — and neither is how it moves.

How to trust this page

Everything on this site lives in a public code repository, and every change — whether you or an automated run made it — is recorded as a timestamped commit that shows exactly what moved, from what value to what value. Because that history is a cryptographic chain, an old entry can't be quietly altered without breaking every record after it. Nothing changes here without a permanent, public, tamper-evident trail.
