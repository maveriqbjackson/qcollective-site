The big picture

Senate: 35 seats were on the ballot. Democrats won 20 and Republicans 13, with Alaska and Mississippi still counting. Democrats flipped 7 seats: Iowa, Kansas, Maine, North Carolina, Ohio, South Carolina and Texas. Republicans flipped none. That gives Democrats a majority of at least 54 in the next Senate, counting the two independents who vote with them.

Governors: 36 states elected a governor. Democrats flipped 6: Alaska, Georgia, Iowa, Nevada, Ohio and Texas. Republicans flipped none. Kansas is still counting.

Colorado: we have every race, down to each state House district, on our full Colorado results page.
