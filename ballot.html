What's on my ballot?

Your ballot depends on where you live. Here's where to see your own, straight from the official sources, in about two minutes.

We don't tell you how to vote. The Q Collective is nonpartisan. We don't endorse candidates or ballot measures, and every link below goes to an official Colorado source.

  1. See your sample ballot

    Go to GoVoteColorado, the Colorado Secretary of State's voter site. Look yourself up and choose View my sample ballot. It shows every race and measure on your ballot, for your address.

    Open GoVoteColorado ↗

  2. Read the plain-English guide to statewide measures

    The Blue Book is written by the legislature's nonpartisan staff. For each statewide measure it gives the full text, a fair and impartial analysis, and the main arguments for and against. It's mailed to voters before each statewide election and posted online.

    Read the Blue Book ↗

  3. Track your ballot

    On GoVoteColorado, choose Sign up for BallotTrax to get a text or email when your ballot is mailed, received and counted. You can also check my mail ballot status there any time.

    Track it on GoVoteColorado ↗

  4. Ask your county clerk

    Your county clerk and recorder runs your election: where to drop off your ballot, where to vote in person, and anything that looks wrong on your ballot. The Secretary of State keeps a contact list for all 64 counties.

    Find your county clerk (PDF) ↗
